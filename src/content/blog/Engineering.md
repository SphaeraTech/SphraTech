Software engineering is a dynamic field that continues to evolve at a rapid pace. As we look ahead to 2025, several trends and advancements are poised to shape the industry. This article explores key areas such as the rise of low-code and no-code platforms, the increasing adoption of microservices architectures, the integration of AI and machine learning in software development, and the growing importance of DevSecOps. We also discuss the skills that software engineers will need to stay competitive in the coming years.
